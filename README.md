# The Resonant Architecture of Cognition

**The original cognitive framework behind The Janus Architecture**

Research and authorship: **Anthony Janus**, independent researcher.

*The Resonant Architecture of Cognition* grew out of a recursive investigation of the author's own cognitive processes, conducted through first-person phenomenological observation and AI-assisted dialogue. The documents develop a relational account of meaning, cognition, and neurodivergence, including the constructs OMEF, FSI, and SCMF.

This repository houses the original research collection as a distinct body of work. That inquiry later informed the development of **The Janus Architecture**, a physical synthetic organism project documented separately.
